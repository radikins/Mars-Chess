How the Catalyst moves

The Catalyst replaces the queenside Bishop for both players.

  • Moves up to three squares in a straight or diagonal line; or
  • Moves along one straight/diagonal direction, makes one 90° turn, then continues.
  • It cannot jump over pieces. It captures only on its final square.
Unlock condition: Each Catalyst is locked until the pawn directly in front of it has actually moved. White's c-pawn unlocks the White Catalyst; Black's c-pawn unlocks the Black Catalyst.
